About GreenAura

An innovative platform built to bridge the gap between community charity demands, pre-loved resource distribution, and carbon-conscious logistics.

Driven by Innovation & Sustainability

GreenAura was founded as an academic design initiative aimed at combating municipal waste generation while reinforcing local community networks. We make sustainability second nature by offering a seamless way to donate items or buy pre-loved electronics, books, and furniture.

By matching regional collection centers with an integrated on-demand courier fulfillment tool, we remove the physical transportation barriers that frequently cause valuable pre-loved materials to go to waste.

Zero Waste Ecosystem

Redirecting usable household items directly away from landfills and into communities where they are needed most.

On-Demand Courier Hook

Removing the friction of transportation logistics by coordinating on-demand local dispatch riders directly via the dashboard.

Transparent Monetization

Sustaining platform operations using micro-transaction delivery service fees and curated pre-loved merchant listings.
